# Fosozu-chan WiFi Maiden 🗼⚡
**The Definitive Four Swords Retropub Edition - V1.4 MASTER**

Welcome to the firewall, Packet-Maiden. Your mission is to maintain the uplink against the aggressive manifestations of the network. Stay synced, stay fast, and avoid the bandwidth throttle in this web-based bullet hell (danmaku) shoot 'em up game!

## 🎮 Core Mechanics

### 1. The Signal Modulation System
Your weapon strength is tied directly to your **GRAZE STREAK**. As you dodge bullets and graze past them, your bandwidth expands:
- **Standard Link (0-4 Streak):** Original wide-spread pattern.
- **High Speed Data (5-9 Streak):** Increased fire rate.
- **Shielded Overclock (10+ Streak):** While the WiFi Shield is active, fire Triple Heavy Beams (Double Damage to bosses).

### 2. WiFi Tether & Border Alarm
The network has physical boundaries. Staying outside triggers the **STALLING PENALTY**:
- Exiting the screen causes the border to **FLASH RED**.
- Staying off-screen > 90 frames = -500 points + auto-tether back to the center of the screen.

### 3. Shield Break & Point of Collection
- **Shield Break Effect:** A cyan shockwave pulses when the graze shield is consumed, providing a brief window of invulnerability.
- **Point of Collection (PoC):** If you move to the top 20% of the screen while holding the Focus button, you will instantly collect all dropped items on the screen!

## 🔄 New Game+ (Link Iterations)
Defeating MADAME SATSUKI triggers a **SERVER RESET**. The game loops back to Wave 1 with these OVERCLOCKED changes:
- **Difficulty:** Bullet velocity +10% and HP +5% per iteration (Capped for stability).
- **Flawless Uplink:** Earn +25,000 points if you clear a boss without breaking your shield.
- **Full Buffer Bonus:** Clear all bosses without using a Reconnect to earn the +50,000 point bonus.
- **Persistence:** High scores and iterations save to Local Storage and survive browser refreshes/F5.

## 🕹️ Controls & Settings
Controls can be fully remapped in the **Settings Menu**. Default controls are:

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move** | Arrow Keys | D-Pad / Left Stick | Navigate your character. |
| **Shoot** | `Z` | Button A | Fire your primary weapon. |
| **Bomb** | `X` | Button B | Clear all bullets on screen and damage enemies (Requires Bomb item). |
| **Focus** | `Shift` | Right Trigger | Slows movement speed and reveals your tiny core hitbox. |
| **Pause** | `Enter` / `Esc` | Start | Opens the Pause Menu. |

*Note: The Settings Menu also contains fully featured Volume Sliders for Master, BGM, and SFX, which are saved across sessions.*

## 🛠️ Dev Controls
If **Dev Controls** are enabled in the Settings Menu (which disables leaderboard submissions), you gain access to QA overrides via the keyboard:
- **`1`**: Instantly increase the NG+ Link Iteration.
- **`2`**: Maximize Power and set Bombs to 9.
- **`3`**: Instantly skip the current wave or instantly kill the active boss.
- **`8`**: QA Warp. Instantly transition to Wave 6 (Madame Satsuki) while forcing a flawless 1CC state to test the endgame transition.
- **`9`**: God Mode Toggle. Ignore all damage and life loss (though audio/visual hit effects still play).
